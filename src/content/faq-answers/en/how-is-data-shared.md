**Bio-GO-SHIP data are deposited in public archives suited to each data type.**

This site does not host data itself. GO-SHIP hydrography is archived at CCHDO, 'omics sequences at NCBI (searchable through Ocean DNA Explorer), optics and pigments at SeaBASS, plankton images at EcoTaxa, species occurrences at OBIS, and project datasets at BCO-DMO and NCEI.

The Data page links to each archive and has a table of archive links for every cruise. A blank cell in that table means nothing has been posted there yet.
