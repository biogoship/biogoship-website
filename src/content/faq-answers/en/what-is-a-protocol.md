**A protocol is a shared method for collecting or processing samples, so measurements can be compared across cruises and over time. The Bio-GO-SHIP protocols shared here allow researchers outside of Bio-GO-SHIP to generate results compatible with the Bio-GO-SHIP program.**

The Protocols page lists the current standard operating procedures (SOPs), each with a short overview and a downloadable file. They cover DNA/RNA sampling and DNA extraction, HPLC pigments, particulate organic matter (POC/N and POP), flow cytometry, inline optics, and the Underwater Vision Profiler (UVP5). The same page has an overview of typical depths, timepoints, and volumes for each sample type.
