**Cite the specific data accessions and DOIs you used, when available.**

Each archive provides its own identifiers, such as an NCBI BioProject or SRA accession, a BCO-DMO dataset DOI, or a CCHDO cruise expocode. The Data page links to the archives for each cruise. If a dataset you need has no DOI yet, contact the team.
