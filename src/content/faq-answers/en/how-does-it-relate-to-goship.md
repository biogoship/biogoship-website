**Bio-GO-SHIP adds biological measurements to GO-SHIP hydrographic sections.**

GO-SHIP repeats ocean transects to track heat, carbon, and other physical and chemical properties. Bio-GO-SHIP samples plankton on those same lines, from the same CTD casts and the ship's underway seawater system, so biology can be compared directly with the physical and chemical context.
