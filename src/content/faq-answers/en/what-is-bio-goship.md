**Bio-GO-SHIP is a community program that measures plankton across the global ocean.**

It quantifies the molecular diversity, size spectrum, chemical composition, and abundances of plankton communities, alongside the physical and chemical measurements made by GO-SHIP. Sampling began on US GO-SHIP cruises in 2015, and the program was officially recognized by NOAA, NASA, and NSF in 2021. It is based at UC Irvine.
