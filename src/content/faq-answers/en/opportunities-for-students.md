**Yes! Training is a core part of Bio-GO-SHIP.**

The program trains undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral students, research associates, and K-12 teachers in oceanographic sampling and analysis. In its pilot phase, 13 students gained hands-on experience at sea and in the lab. If you are interested, contact the team.
