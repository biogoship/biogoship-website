### Overview

GO-SHIP P16S (Leg 1) runs along roughly 150°W in the South Pacific aboard the R/V Thomas G. Thompson (University of Washington), from January 14 to March 6, 2027, for 53 days at sea. Mobilization takes place January 10–13, 2027.

- **Start:** Auckland, New Zealand (Port of Auckland, Captain Cook Wharf)
- **End:** Papeete, Tahiti

### Leadership

- **Chief Scientist:** Leah Chomiak (U. Miami CIMAS / NOAA AOML)
- **Co-Chief Scientist:** Steffi O'Daly (UW CICOES)
- **Captain:** Eric Haroldson

### Ship and crew

The R/V Thomas G. Thompson operates at full science capacity with about 34 science berths, including marine technician and medical staff support.

### Operations

- About 108 core GO-SHIP stations spanning 71°S to 17.5°S.
- About 25 Bio-GO-SHIP casts.
- **Core measurements:** CTD/rosette, bottle salts, dissolved oxygen, nutrients, Total CO₂ (DIC), underway pCO₂, total alkalinity, pH, CFC-11, CFC-12, SF₆, Lowered ADCP (LADCP), and Shipboard ADCP (SADCP).
- **Level 2 and Level 3 measurements:** Δ¹⁴C and δ¹³C of DIC, N₂O concentration, Bio-GO-SHIP (pigments, flow cytometry, imaging, eDNA), CTD transmissometer, N₂O isotopocules, Underwater Vision Profiler (UVP), CTD FDOM suite, ammonium, aerosols, and water isotopes (δ¹⁸O).
- **Platform deployments:** BGC-Argo floats, Deep Argo floats, and surface drifters.

### Planning information

**Data management and archival.** All core hydrographic data are processed and submitted to the [CCHDO](https://cchdo.ucsd.edu/) (CLIVAR and Carbon Hydrographic Data Office) following standard GO-SHIP guidelines.

**Logistics and compliance.**

- Strict biosecurity regulations apply (e.g., New Zealand biosecurity clearance, ISPM-15 compliant treated wood standards for shipping containers).
- All participating personnel must complete required onboarding profiles (such as UNOLS Marine Facilities Planning) and review the US GO-SHIP Code of Conduct.
- Mandatory pre-cruise training (such as joint Bystander Intervention Training) is coordinated ahead of field operations.
